Videos Tag

To embed videos in HTML, you can use the

Attributes for Video

We can use the following attributes:
  1. src: Specifies the URL of the video file.
  2. width: Adjusts the width of the video player. Height adjusts automatically to maintain aspect ratio.
  3. controls: Displays video controls such as play, pause, volume, etc.
  4. autoplay: Automatically starts playing the video when the page loads.
  5. loop: Causes the video to automatically start over from beginning when it reaches the end.
  6. preload: Specifies whether the video should be loaded when the page loads (auto, metadata, none).
